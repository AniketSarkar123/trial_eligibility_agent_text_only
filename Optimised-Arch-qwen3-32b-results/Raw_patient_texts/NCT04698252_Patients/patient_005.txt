Ms. Evans is a 71-year-old postmenopausal patient with ER+/HER2- breast cancer. She has oligometastatic disease restricted to 2 cervical lymph nodes. She has been receiving systemic letrozole for the past 9 months, achieving a documented partial response. Staging PET/CT confirms no other distant metastases. She is fully independent (ECOG 0) and has no cardiac or pulmonary comorbidities. Bloodwork demonstrates adequate bone marrow reserve and normal hepatic function. She is ready for localized surgical excision of the involved nodes.
