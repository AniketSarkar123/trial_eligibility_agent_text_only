Ms. Irwin, 33, was diagnosed with unifocal TNBC. Because of her young age, she was given a 12-week course of neoadjuvant chemotherapy before her lumpectomy. Her surgical pathology showed a tremendous response, with a residual pT1b tumor (1.2 cm) and completely negative nodes (pN0). Her sTIL score on the surgical specimen was 85%. She has no other cancer history.
