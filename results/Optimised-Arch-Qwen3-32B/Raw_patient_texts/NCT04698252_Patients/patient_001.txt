Ms. Alvarez is a 62-year-old postmenopausal woman diagnosed with ER+ (95%), PR+ (80%), HER2- breast cancer. She presented with de novo oligometastatic disease featuring three distinct bone lesions in the thoracic spine and right femur. She has been treated with letrozole and ribociclib for 8 months. Recent restaging CT and bone scans confirm stable disease with no new metastatic sites and partial sclerosis of the bone lesions. Her ECOG performance status is 0. Her liver and renal function tests are completely normal. She is being evaluated for stereotactic body radiation to her bone metastases.
