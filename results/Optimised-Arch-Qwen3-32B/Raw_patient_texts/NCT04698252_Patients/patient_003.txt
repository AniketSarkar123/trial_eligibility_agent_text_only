Ms. Carter is a 68-year-old female with ER-positive (100%), PR-negative, HER2-negative metastatic breast cancer. Her disease is limited to a single 2.1 cm lung nodule in the right lower lobe (oligometastatic). After 10 months on an aromatase inhibitor combined with palbociclib, her chest CT shows excellent stable disease with no progression or additional nodules. She has an ECOG of 0, normal left ventricular ejection fraction, and pristine baseline laboratory values. She is highly active and is a prime candidate for a localized wedge resection.
